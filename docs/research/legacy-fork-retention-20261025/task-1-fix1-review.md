## Cost and remaining nonblocking limits

The authority preflight is streamed and bounded in resident graph metadata, but it is not cheap: startup scans every final application graph and reads both expiry slots for every observation, including repeated observations. An expiry prune first performs a whole preflight and then `expireLegacy` repeats that whole graph/slot scan for each expiring transferred ref. With E such refs and R retained observations across applications, that validation component can perform O(E×R) repeated observation/slot work within the original L0 mutation turn. Service transitions and other writers must join/wait for that original work. No expensive startup/many-expiring-ref measurement was performed in this round, so104 is not a scale or latency qualification for the new preflight.
