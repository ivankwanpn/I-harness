## 1. Require the shared-budget concurrency control, without presuming a defect

The exposed service already permits a legacy capture/grant and managed begin/write/finish concurrently. Legacy work uses `service.ts:31–35,51–60` mutation/job lanes; managed work uses `artifact/ownership.ts`'s separate workspace mutate/OS lock. The stated one physical workspace budget applies to that supported composition. Sequential totals alone cannot close it.

Make one deterministic mixed-admission scheduling control mandatory after sequential positives. Calibrate the real legacy pre/post accounting/write boundaries (`service.ts:139`, `store.ts:187–193,209–230`) and managed requireSpace/write boundary; keep a fixed configured limit and an independently measured initial occupancy. Delegate actual filesystem/SQLite work. Record each operation's full/partial/refused/unknown disposition, protected-file invariants, remaining temporary bytes and measured native boundaries. Conservative refusal or rollback of only the new legacy item can be correct; a race is not automatically a product RED.

Schedule only at actual asynchronous native boundaries. SQLite DatabaseSync/index transactions are synchronous: do not block inside them waiting for another same-thread operation to advance. Nor call a queued service method recursively from its held lane. `configure`/`clear`/`close` abort and drain legacy jobs (`service.ts:64–79`); they are separate transition controls, not neutral scheduling barriers for the admission race. Always release held native I/O in finally before joining draining cleanup. The original quota scan's global32 metadata-I/O test qualifies accounting concurrency, not this admission interleaving.

The draft asks for exact peak totals. Boundary snapshots prove measured peaks; they do not prove the maximum of unobserved SQLite journal writes. Label sampled/boundary peaks honestly unless complete native observation establishes a true peak. State which documented temporary allowance, if any, belongs to the contract before classifying an overage; do not invent a new allowance to make a failed control pass. Any demonstrated correction still goes to root first; no locking design is approved here.
