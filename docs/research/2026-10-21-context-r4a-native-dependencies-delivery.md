Parent target deletion is interrupted after actual pin removal, then retried through the original coordinator; missing-pin retry removes companions and the last-owned frame. A separate storage-owner control verifies another target's companion/pin remains usable when one target is deleted.

The four-artifact composition independently returns over 70KB of deterministic primary metadata, crossing the actual 60,000-byte inline threshold, plus two distinct actual native frames and one catalog.
