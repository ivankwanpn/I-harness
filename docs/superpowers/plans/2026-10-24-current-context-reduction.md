## C4R — preferred refinement: complete projected source through bounded recorded pages

**Why prefer paging:** head/tail observes only512 bytes per result and can permanently omit a unique fact in the middle before the summarizer ever sees it. Host-accessible original storage does not repair that model information loss automatically. Paging gives every portable projected text byte to some successful summary call and tests useful facts surviving the bounded rolling checkpoint. It costs several calls/latency and still cannot guarantee perfect natural-model recall; observed-input coverage and summary accuracy are separate claims. If exhaustive paging cannot finish under existing bounds, block without replacement rather than silently switch to excerpts.
